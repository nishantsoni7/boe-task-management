# Payments: a one-page guide for PI approvers

**Remember one sentence:** can it be fixed? Return it to Sales. Is the deal off? Close it.

## Your two choices for a PI with a problem

| Button | Use it when | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Return to Sales** | Anything that can be fixed: wrong item, price, file or detail. | Sales correct **this same PI** and resubmit it. Its payments stay on it and stay verified. Nothing has to be uploaded again. |
| **Close: deal lost** | The client isn't going ahead. | The PI is closed for good. Its payments go back to the client's money, ready for their next PI or Order. The payment records aren't changed, so verified money stays verified. |

Both choices need a reason. The window shows how much money is on the PI before you confirm.

If you're unsure, choose **Return to Sales**. A closed PI can't be reopened.

## What you'll see on the PI

- **Returned to Sales:** waiting for Sales to fix it and resubmit.
- **Closed (deal lost):** final. The history shows who closed it, when and why.

## Approving

A PI cannot be approved while any payment on it is undecided. An HDFC payment is verified against the bank, in Payment Requests, so ask Finance if one is holding the PI.

Otherwise nothing changes: when the advance rules are met, **Approve PI & Create Order** works as before. Payments on the PI move onto the new Order automatically.
