# Payments: a one-page guide for Sales

**Remember one sentence:** type the amount and date first. If your money shows up, tap **Use this money**. If not, record it with the UTR.

## Adding a payment

1. Open the PI (or the Order) and tap **Add payment**.
2. Type the **amount** and the **date** the client paid, and choose the account.
3. Wait a moment. If the company has already received this money from this client, a box appears: **Money already received from this client**.
   - **It's there?** Tap **Use this money**. The payment moves onto your PI with the same Payment ID. If Finance had already verified it, it stays verified and counts straight away.
   - **It's not there?** Carry on and fill in the rest. Add the **UTR** (the transaction number on the client's screenshot or bank SMS) and attach the screenshot.
4. Tap **Record payment**.

Never record the same transfer twice. If you're not sure, ask Finance before you record it.

## Reading a payment

Every payment shows a small tracker:

| You see | It means |
|---|---|
| Recorded → **Waiting for Finance** (amber) | Finance hasn't checked it yet. It doesn't count yet. |
| Recorded → **Verified** (green) | Finance checked it. It counts toward the 40% advance. |
| Recorded → **Needs Clarification** (amber) | Finance asked you a question. Open the payment and answer it. |
| Recorded → **Rejected** (red) | Finance didn't accept it. The reason is shown. |

## When your PI comes back

- **Returned to Sales:** fix the same PI (you can replace the workbook) and tap **Resubmit for Approval**. The payments are still on it. Don't add them again.
- **Closed (deal lost):** the PI is final. Its payments go back to the client's money. If the client comes back later, make a new PI. When you add a payment there, the old money shows up, and you tap **Use this money**.
